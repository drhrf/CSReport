Your paper needs a buddy

Using companion statistical reports

Open the slides → The R code on the slides runs in your browser. Wait for the yellow “Downloading webR” badge to disappear, then press Run. Download all class materials (.zip) Word template, filled example, Quarto demo and data.

Files

CSR_Template_Word.docx Blank companion statistical report in Word. Replace each grey sentence with your own text. Track A of the exercise.
CSR_Example_WaterMaze.docx The same template, filled in for the water-maze example. Keep it open as a model.
csr_demo.qmd Executable CSR in Quarto (base R only, no packages to install). Open in RStudio or Positron next to maze.csv and click Render. Track B.
maze.csv Simulated data: 12 mice, 4 water-maze trials each.
csr_demo.html What csr_demo.qmd looks like after rendering (opens in the browser).
csr_demo.docx The same rendered report, as a Word file.

No R on your computer?

For Track B you can use a free account at posit.cloud: create a new RStudio project, upload csr_demo.qmd and maze.csv, open the .qmd and click Render. Track A only needs Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs.
